# Input schema

The repository does not contain participant-level data. The analysis scripts expect frozen, de-identified analysis tables prepared under the relevant UK Biobank access approval.

## LE8 scoring source table

`python/cohort/01_score_le8.py` accepts a prepared `.dta` or `.csv` table with one row per participant and the source columns listed in the script's `SOURCE_COLUMNS` constant. These include the 16 prespecified Oxford WebQ-derived dietary item indicators, physical activity, smoking/cessation and passive exposure, sleep duration, BMI, non-HDL cholesterol, fasting glucose, HbA1c, diabetes status, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, and antihypertensive medication use. The diet component is mapped to the 0–100 LE8 component scale.

The output contract uses `LE8`, `LE8_legacy_do`, `le8_diet_primary`, `le8_pa`, `le8_nicotine`, `le8_sleep`, `le8_bmi`, `le8_nonhdl`, `le8_glucose`, and `le8_bp`, matching the downstream R analysis scripts.

## Full-cohort table

The full-cohort CSV must contain the participant identifier and the frozen variables used by the primary CKD analysis, including the LE8 total and component scores, follow-up time, incident CKD indicator, baseline eGFR/UACR, WebQ timing and count, age, sex, ethnicity, deprivation, education-deprivation, assessment center, and energy intake. The exact column names are checked by each script's command-line stage; do not silently rename fields without updating the corresponding script.

## MRI-cohort table

The MRI-cohort CSV must contain participant identifier, MRI age, chronological age, total and component LE8 scores, out-of-sample combined/T1/diffusion BAG or prediction columns, pre-MRI WebQ timing and energy intake, MRI center, ethnicity, deprivation, education-deprivation, and the prespecified MRI acquisition/quality-control covariates.

## Prediction and feature tables

Brain-age prediction files must preserve participant identifiers and the exact feature ordering used during training. White-matter localization additionally requires a feature table and a feature-to-tract/metric mapping table. These files are restricted UK Biobank-derived data and must be stored locally, not committed to GitHub.

## Brain-age split label

`R/brain_age/20_assign_brain_age_splits.R` reconstructs the fixed model-development labels. It accepts either the prepared `healthy` indicator used by the original model-development analysis or a one-row-per-participant table containing `participant_id`, `age`, `mri_date`, `earliest_inpatient_icd10_date`, `long_standing_illness`, and `self_rated_health`. Health status is evaluated at the Instance 2 MRI examination. A participant is eligible for the healthy group only when no inpatient ICD-10 diagnosis is recorded on or before MRI, no long-standing illness, disability, or infirmity is reported, and self-rated health is excellent or good. Participants with complete information who fail at least one criterion are assigned to `Non-healthy test`; participants with incomplete required information are assigned to `Health status unknown`.

The 6,887 eligible healthy participants are divided into `Healthy train` (n=5,507) and `Healthy validation` (n=1,380) using 10 age strata defined by type-2 sample quantiles, a 20% stratum-specific validation fraction, R seed 20260714, and the original row order. The remaining labels are `Non-healthy test` (n=32,216) and `Health status unknown` (n=966). The script enforces these published counts.

The training-stage `candidate_predictions_all.csv` used for Figure 2 panels C through G must contain `age`, `split`, `pred_raw__lasso__featurewiz`, `pred_corrected__lasso__featurewiz`, and `bag__lasso__featurewiz`. This participant-level file remains inside the approved UK Biobank environment and is not included in the public repository.

## Figure inputs

`figures/02_make_figures_v2.py` expects the following files under `<figure_table_root>/tables/supplement/`:

- `cohort_flow_detailed.csv`
- `Table_3_LE8_component_profiles.csv`
- `Table_P9_standardized_modality_specific_slopes.csv`
- `Table_P10_continuous_BP_glycaemia_modality_slopes.csv`
- `Table_P11_diffusion_metric_composites.csv`
- `Table_P11_significant_tracts_FDR134.csv`
- `Table_P12_sex_specific_slopes.csv`
- `modality_model_performance.csv`
- `Table_P4_CKD_adjusted_FG_CIF.csv`

## Variable provenance

Consult the final manuscript supplement for the source-field and assessment-instance map, and verify its agreement with the cohort-generation code. Table numbering may change between submission versions.

Before the R stages, `run_pipeline.ps1` applies `python/kidney/egfr_ckd_epi_2021.py` to the full and MRI cohort tables. The utility converts UK Biobank serum creatinine from µmol/L to mg/dL, calculates `egfr_2021_ckd_epi_cr_cys`, rebuilds UACR in mg/g, and writes the identical compatibility aliases `egfr_cys_do` and `uacr` used by the R scripts.

`python/kidney/23_build_composite_ckd_endpoint.py` requires a participant index/censoring table, one code-qualified CKD event table combining primary-care, hospital, and death records, and a longitudinal kidney-laboratory table. The code-qualified event table has one row per qualifying record with `participant_id` and `event_date`. The laboratory table has `participant_id`, `measurement_date`, `egfr`, and `uacr`. The program identifies laboratory CKD from two abnormal values of the same marker at least 90 days apart, combines all sources by the earliest qualifying date, excludes prevalent CKD, and returns one composite endpoint.
